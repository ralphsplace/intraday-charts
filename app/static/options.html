How to read it. Bid, ask and implied volatility are snapshots from TWS (US options market data needed), taken when you ask. Values before an expiry are estimates from Black-Scholes with each leg's own implied volatility (no dividends, European exercise): real prices also move with volatility and skew. The chance of profit assumes a lognormal move at the front expiry's at-the-money implied volatility.
