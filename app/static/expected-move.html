How to read it. The straddle (at-the-money call + put, mid prices, at the stock's price) is the market's average expected move by the expiry's 16:00 close. 0.85 × straddle is a common rule of thumb. 1σ (about a two-in-three chance of closing inside) and 2σ come from implied volatility and the calendar time left. When implied volatility jumps between the previous expiry and this one, the options price an event between them, usually earnings; its own move is shown separately.

Diagonal legs: short call and put at the listed strikes nearest the expected move in this expiry, long call and put one strike closer to the money in the next weekly expiry; net = long − short per side. Prices only; nothing here is a signal or advice, and the app never places orders.

Quotes are snapshots from TWS (US options market data needed), refreshed when you ask. Outside regular hours they are often wide or stale; the page says so.
